A program entry for make.fun's **"Make the AI Judge Laugh: the Office Joke Open"** bounty ($2 in USDC).

## What it does

`main.py` writes one line of joke about office life to `/output/entry.txt`:

> HR said to bring my whole self to work, so I brought my resignation letter too.

The setup is a real corporate platitude every office worker has heard;
the turn is the one thing nobody is supposed to bring — a resignation
letter.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

The program is fully deterministic: the same 79-character line is written
on every run. It takes no input, makes no network calls, and needs no
dependencies beyond Python 3.

## The line

Sixteen candidate lines were drafted and scored against the competition's
published judge brief — a genuine one-line office joke first, then a real
turn or surprise at the end, then recognition (the truth of the office),
then tight wording, then freshness. The HR platitude won: it is a real joke
with a punchline, specific to office life, plain on first read, and a fresh
angle — no printer, no Monday morning, no reply-all, no
meeting-could-have-been-an-email.
